AnkiBlitz Core — session discipline, without the reveal timers

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT IT DOES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

AnkiBlitz Core is the lean build of AnkiBlitz: focused, timed study with real
structure, but WITHOUT the card-reveal timers. One engine, one menu, one
settings window:

• Blitz sessions — focused, timed runs (by card count, time, or a fraction of
  what's due) with a progress bar and a completion screen. The bar stays up
  outside a Blitz too, dimmed, filling against everything you have due — so
  plain reviewing isn't a blank screen.

• Pomodoro — chain work blocks with short and long breaks, a break screen with
  journaling, focus ratings and a guided breathing pacer, and configurable
  auto-return.

• Focus Lock — make leaving a session progressively harder (confirm → min-cards
  → finish), with an optional Focus Score. Pick the level per Blitz in the Start
  dialog without changing your saved default.

• In-app music — a SoundCloud / YouTube Music player on the break screen, as a
  floating review dock, and on the deck list.

PROFILES
Three built-ins — Default (everyday), Blitz (a committed, locked-in run), and
Relaxed (low friction) — flip the *feel* in one click: Focus Lock, the near-end
nudge, anti-pressure display. They deliberately leave your session shape (you
pick mode/target each launch) and music alone. Save your own, too; your current
setup is kept as a "My setup" profile so nothing is lost.

WANT REVEAL PACING TOO?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Core leaves out the card-reveal timers on purpose. Two companion add-ons add
them and run alongside Core:
  • Adaptive Speed Focus (aSFM) — adaptive auto-reveal timer, with a "More time"
    button: https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/1148593203
  • Progressive Word Reveal — fades the question in word by word: https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/972193513
Or install the all-in-one AnkiBlitz, which bundles everything: https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/178722601

CHANGES IN 1.2.0
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
• NEW: the progress bar now stays up outside a Blitz, dimmed and labelled "All
  due", filling against your whole due pile. Its target is live, so a card that
  comes back in learning pushes it out by one — it fills exactly as your queue
  empties, never before. No accuracy, Again count or streak on it, ever.
• NEW: Start Blitz… lets you pick a Focus Lock level for that one session —
  commit hard to a session you need to finish, or let yourself off for a tired
  one, without changing your saved default.
• NEW: a guided breathing pacer on the break screen. Collapsed as a "Breathe"
  button until you press it; Box 4-4-4-4, Simple 4-4, Coherent 5-5 or Calming
  4-7-8.
• NEW: the break screen's "Step away" button and floating countdown, break audio
  muting, and resuming an unfinished run from earlier the same day.
• FIX: Focus Lock level 3 now keeps a Blitz alive on focus loss based on the
  level that Blitz was started with, not whatever is currently configured.
• FIX: "Finish all due" on the deck-list panel no longer swallows the click.
