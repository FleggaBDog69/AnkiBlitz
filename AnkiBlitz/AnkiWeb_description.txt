AnkiBlitz — pace your reviews, not your grades

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT IT DOES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

AnkiBlitz rolls three review-pacing ideas into one engine, one menu and one
settings window:

• Adaptive auto-reveal — after the question shows, AnkiBlitz waits a delay
  computed from the card's length and FSRS difficulty, then reveals the answer.
  It NEVER grades for you — you still press the button. Picture/visual note
  types or decks can use a SET time instead (no word count), since there's
  nothing to read.

• Progressive word reveal — the question fades in word by word at a reading pace
  you set, optionally locked to the card's {{tts}} voice. Skipping ahead stops
  the card's audio too — you've read it, so the voice reading it to you is just
  noise.

• Blitz sessions — focused, timed runs (by card count, time, or a fraction of
  what's due) with a progress bar, plus Pomodoro work/break cycles, Focus Lock,
  momentum nudges, an in-app music player, and whole-config Profiles. The
  progress bar stays up outside a Blitz too, dimmed, filling against everything
  you have due — so plain reviewing isn't a blank screen.

• Hold the timer whenever you like — a "More time" button on the card, or a
  pause key (default P). Either stops the countdown where it stands and picks it
  up where it stopped.

PROFILES
Three built-ins — Default (everyday), Blitz (a committed, locked-in run), and
Relaxed (low friction) — flip the *feel* in one click: auto-reveal pace, Focus
Lock, the near-end nudge, anti-pressure display. They deliberately leave your
session shape (you pick mode/target each launch), word-reveal speed and music
alone. The picker shows each profile's key settings before you apply. Save your
own, too; your current setup is kept as a "My setup" profile so nothing is lost.

BEFORE YOU START
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

AnkiBlitz bundles its own versions of these, so DISABLE them to avoid double
timers (Tools ▸ Add-ons):
  • Speed Focus Mode
  • Progressive Word Reveal
  • Sprint Mode
A one-time welcome wizard reminds you on first launch.

THE ANKIBLITZ SUITE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Want only one piece? Each half ships on its own:
  • AnkiBlitz Core — sessions, Pomodoro, Focus Lock, profiles, music, WITHOUT
    the two reveal timers: https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/1174429600
  • Adaptive Speed Focus (aSFM) — the auto-reveal timer alone: https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/1148593203
  • Progressive Word Reveal — the word-by-word fade alone: https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/972193513

Core + aSFM + Progressive Word Reveal together equal this add-on. Don't run any
of them alongside AnkiBlitz — it already contains all three, and the timers
would double up.

CHANGES IN 1.2.0
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
• NEW: a "More time" button on the card, by request — the same hold the pause
  key applies, but you can see that it's there. Click to stop the countdown,
  click again (or the "paused" badge) to carry on.
• NEW: a pause key (default P) for the same hold, from the keyboard.
• NEW: the progress bar now stays up outside a Blitz, dimmed and labelled "All
  due", filling against your whole due pile. Its target is live, so a card that
  comes back in learning pushes it out by one — it fills exactly as your queue
  empties, never before. No accuracy, Again count or streak on it, ever.
• NEW: Start Blitz… lets you pick a Focus Lock level for that one session —
  commit hard to a session you need to finish, or let yourself off for a tired
  one, without changing your saved default.
• NEW: a guided breathing pacer on the Pomodoro break screen. Collapsed as a
  "Breathe" button until you press it; Box 4-4-4-4, Simple 4-4, Coherent 5-5 or
  Calming 4-7-8.
• NEW: skipping the word reveal also stops the card's TTS / [sound:] playback.
• NEW: matches SynapsePro's theme when that add-on is installed.
• FIX: the reveal key no longer fires while you're typing in Anki's type-in-the-
  answer box, or with Ctrl/Cmd/Alt held.
• FIX: "Finish all due" on the deck-list panel no longer swallows the click.
