AnkiBlitz — pace your reviews, not your grades

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT IT DOES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

AnkiBlitz rolls three review-pacing ideas into one engine, one menu and one
settings window:

• Adaptive auto-reveal — after the question shows, AnkiBlitz waits a delay
  computed from the card's length and FSRS difficulty, then reveals the answer.
  It NEVER grades for you — you still press the button. Picture/visual note
  types or decks can use a SET time instead (no word count), since there's
  nothing to read.

• Progressive word reveal — the question fades in word by word at a reading pace
  you set, optionally locked to the card's {{tts}} voice.

• Blitz sessions — focused, timed runs (by card count, time, or a fraction of
  what's due) with a progress bar, plus Pomodoro work/break cycles, Focus Lock,
  momentum nudges, an in-app music player, and whole-config Profiles.

PROFILES
Three built-ins — Default (everyday), Blitz (a committed, locked-in run), and
Relaxed (low friction) — flip the *feel* in one click: auto-reveal pace, Focus
Lock, the near-end nudge, anti-pressure display. They deliberately leave your
session shape (you pick mode/target each launch), word-reveal speed and music
alone. The picker shows each profile's key settings before you apply. Save your
own, too; your current setup is kept as a "My setup" profile so nothing is lost.

BEFORE YOU START
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

AnkiBlitz bundles its own versions of these, so DISABLE them to avoid double
timers (Tools ▸ Add-ons):
  • Speed Focus Mode
  • Progressive Word Reveal
  • Sprint Mode
A one-time welcome wizard reminds you on first launch.

NOTES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
• Works standalone. Your stats and settings are preserved across updates.
• Word-count timing counts only the text you actually see — hidden fields and
  tag chips on AnKing-style notes don't inflate the timer.
• Bundles concepts from Glutanimate's Speed Focus Mode, Progressive Word Reveal
  and Sprint Mode; licensed under the GNU AGPLv3.
• Tested on Anki 2.1.50+ (Qt6).
